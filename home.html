THEME EXPLANATION
“

Pallidars: The Primal Source of Knowledge honors the Islamic historic mosque-based learning centers as the original wellspring of wisdom, scholarship, and moral character. Before modern institutions emerged, these traditional study circles nurtured generations of thinkers in theology, literature, and philosophy; serving as the foundational bedrock of our community's enduring intellectual and cultural heritage.
